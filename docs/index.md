# GKS Starter Kit

Real-world walk-throughs of high-value uses of the GA4GH Genomic Knowledge Standards.

The Starter Kit is for anyone evaluating, advocating for, or starting to build on GKS. Each vignette describes a real implementation: the problem it solves, the data, the tools, and what GKS unlocks — written so you can share it with your team or your leadership.
