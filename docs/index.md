# Genomic Knowledge Model Starter Kit

The **Genomic Knowledge Model (GKM) Starter Kit** was developed to give the community
a **clear, practical entry point for understanding and adopting GKM standards**. GKM is
a collection of standards that define shared models for representing genomic variation
and related knowledge, providing a common foundation for how this information is
structured and exchanged. The Starter Kit brings together
**real-world implementations, reusable resources**, and **examples** that show how GKM is
being used in practice and help others apply those approaches to their own projects.

The Starter Kit is organized around
**three pillars that support GKM adoption in practice**:
