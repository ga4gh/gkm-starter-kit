Genomic Knowledge Model Starter Kit

The Genomic Knowledge Model (GKM) Starter Kit was developed to give the community a clear, practical entry point for understanding and adopting GKM standards. It brings together real-world implementations, reusable resources, and examples that show how GKM is being used in practice and help others apply those approaches to their own projects.

The Starter Kit is organized around three pillars that support GKM adoption in practice:
